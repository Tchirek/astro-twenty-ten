Every useful archive starts with a first note. This site is for things I want to find again: commands that saved an afternoon, explanations that finally clicked, and the occasional observation from outside the terminal.

## What belongs here

- Technical notes with enough context to remain useful.
- Small experiments, including the failures.
- Links accompanied by an explanation, not merely a bookmark.

## A promise to future me

URLs should stay put, prose should remain readable, and the site should never need a loading spinner to show a paragraph.
